## Classic creature assets

`apps/web/public/models/classic/*.glb` and the companion PNG pixel atlases are original repository-authored geometry, UVs, pixel colors and animation curves, offered under Apache-2.0. The reproducible Blender source and production instructions are in `scripts/assets/classic-creatures/`. `manifest.json` records the recipe, tool version and per-asset hashes. No Minecraft or third-party artwork is distributed. The retired skinned actor above remains solely a generic importer test fixture and is not a Classic species or public sample.

Classic art direction is documented in [Classic visual style](docs/classic-visual-style.md). Runtime textures remain editable source assets; user imports retain their own licenses.
