Unknown, wrong-key and mixed-revision windows remain rejected; voxel semantics remain live. The original general reader is unchanged. An adjacent gameplay-cell helper was extracted to keep GameServer within its existing 500-line gate, retaining its instance reader, unknown short-circuit and fluid call order.
